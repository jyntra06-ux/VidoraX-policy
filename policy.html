Privacy Policy

App: VidoraX – Video Player
Last Updated: 29-09-2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the VidoraX – Video Player mobile application ("App", "we", "us", or "our").

VidoraX – Video Player provides features for downloading, managing, browsing, and playing supported video content. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be accessed, collected, processed, or shared when you use the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Permissions Used by the App

The App uses Android permissions to provide its features and supporting functionality.

Internet Access

android.permission.INTERNET

Internet access is used for features such as user-initiated video downloads, accessing online resources, displaying advertisements, retrieving remote configuration, analytics, crash reporting, and performance monitoring.

Network State

android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

The App may check the device's network connection so that features requiring internet access can operate correctly.

Video Access

android.permission.READ_MEDIA_VIDEO

On supported Android versions, the App may request permission to access video files stored on your device.

This access is used to provide video-library and media-player features, such as discovering, displaying, organizing, selecting, and playing video files stored on your device.

The App does not use this permission to upload your personal video files for advertising purposes.

You can grant or deny this permission through Android's permission controls. Some video-library functionality may not work if permission is denied.

Legacy Storage Permissions

android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE

On older supported Android versions, legacy storage permissions may be used to access or save video files.

These permissions are limited to Android versions where legacy storage access is applicable and are not requested on modern Android versions where they are no longer required.

Foreground Service

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE
android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC

The App may use an Android foreground service for user-initiated operations such as active video downloads or related data-transfer operations.

Android may display a foreground-service notification while such an operation is running.

2. Locally Stored Information

The App may store certain information locally on your device to provide its features and remember your preferences.
